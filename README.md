# 🛡️ Incident Response & Forensic Readiness Plan — Yorkuni Leisure Group (YULG)

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project presents a professionally developed **Incident Response and Forensic Readiness Plan** tailored for a fictional small-to-medium enterprise, **Yorkuni Leisure Group (YULG)**, which experienced a major malware outbreak. 
This project simulates a real-world malware outbreak scenario affecting a small-to-medium enterprise, **Yorkuni Leisure Group (YULG)**. The focus is on designing an effective **Forensic Response Plan** that strengthens the company's ability to detect, respond to, and recover from future incidents, while preserving digital evidence for post-incident investigation.

The objective is to strengthen YULG's cyber resilience through clear **pre-incident preparation** steps that i have prepared and a **structured response strategy** that maximizes digital evidence preservation and minimizes operational disruption during future incidents. I have also quoted relevant sections of ISO standards in my responses for integrity. As this is my belief of necessary steps to take, I understand that there might be more effective steps out there and am open to dialogue.

---

## 🧠 Purpose

- Improve organizational **cybersecurity preparedness**
- Establish clear **forensic procedures** for potential security breaches
- Enhance response capability in line with industry frameworks (e.g., **NIST**, **ISO**)
- Help leadership teams make **informed decisions** during incident response
